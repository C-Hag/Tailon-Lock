Briefing

  • Win by shooting down every enemy jet three times, or by destroying all six of their ground targets.
  • Co-op: fly as wingmen against a CPU squadron with one jet per player. Online, the host's Match and Enemy pilot settings apply to both computers.
  • Keep the enemy in your sight until the diamond turns red, then fire a missile. Missiles also lock onto ground targets.
  • Flares can decoy an incoming missile. A hard turn can break its lock.
  • Refuel and rearm behind the circling tanker at matching speed, or with a slow pass below 500 ft over your own runway.
  • SAM sites guard each base. Come in fast and low, or take them out first.
  • Music: "Carrier Dawn", an original SID-style theme. Click anywhere to start the sound.
