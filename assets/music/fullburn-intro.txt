fullburn-intro.wav: the opening film's music, a cut of the main theme, Full Burn "Arp sound DMX10"
(music/fullburn/), from its ear-safe mix (the bell clock softened, the lead's 2.4 kHz dip, -20 LUFS). Half a second of silence, then bars 1-40 of the
song (6-frame sixteenths on the PAL clock, 1.9152 s a bar), faded over 2.5 s; 32 kHz stereo. The same
cut and timing as the DMX3 version it replaces, so the film's sync points are unchanged. The game plays
it from the recording instead of a score (music.c, themes with a .wav), once, and the menu's own theme
takes over when it ends.

Intro version 9 (2026-11-05): the film plays the score (fullburn-intro.json), not this recording, and that score
repeats 8 bars of the song's intro (bars 1-4, 3-4, 3-4, 5-6, 5-6, 5-6, then 7 on), so the band hit is at 27.31 s. This
recording keeps the old 66 s cut and is out of step with the version 9 film. The game never plays it while
fullburn-intro.json is there: a score always wins over a recording of the same theme (music.c, load_recording).
